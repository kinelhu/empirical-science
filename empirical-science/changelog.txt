Version: 0.72.0
Date: 2026-10-10
  Changes:
    - Analysis stations read one sample every 5 seconds instead of every half second. A large lab needs more than one station.
    - The Screening grid's Pays? column is now Net: the gain after the additive's own cost at the best dose, coloured like the other columns. Hover it for the samples still needed. The Dose column is renamed Best dose.
    - Best dose is the tested dose whose yield gain has the highest lower bound, not the highest mean, so it no longer jumps between doses as samples arrive. The port's best dose follows it.
    - A dosed machine's panel shows what it made with its dose and how many of those the dose added, counted from when the dose was set.
    - New Screening column, Packs per sample: the empirical science packs the next sample of each additive makes.
    - The station port has four pages, every value on a signal whose icon says what it is: Results, Screening (Lab circuits 2), Selected result (Lab circuits 2) and Best composition (Lab circuits 3). The letter signals are gone. An additive's signal on the station's input selects it on every page, which adds its dose: a lab can choose what to screen next, and at what dose, from one page. The Station signals tip lists the pages.
    - The Empirical Science screen lists an additive once you can make it or have evidence on it, as the dose picker already did.
    - The station window shows Unreadable and Failed trials only once they count something.
    - Samples are one item per science pack, the pack drawn on the cover: an automation science pack sample, a logistic science pack sample, and so on. Samples in a save become their kind.
    - An analysis station takes the samples of the science packs it holds, and holds four waiting. A station with red and green packs splits its waiting slots between their samples. Stations along a belt now share its samples.
    - Analysis stations are smaller: four slots for waiting samples, two for empirical science packs, one per science pack, and scrap samples' slots with Space Age. Station blueprints from before 0.72 lose the station, and science packs held in a station from before 0.72 may be lost.
    - Analysis stations show the lab's working animation while they analyse.
    - Alternative recipes are removed, with their technology, trials and adoption. Machines set to one go back to having no recipe. Maps keep their hidden world.
    - Analysis precision 1 costs half as much, so a first study pays for it.
    - Per-target productivity research is removed. Levels researched in a save are lost with it. Research data now joins the packs of vanilla's endless technologies, such as mining productivity, worker robot speed and weapon damage.
    - Research data from a question now falls with all its samples, at every dose: testing each dose once no longer pays the full rate on every sample. A set of additives earns the least until each of them has been tested alone on that target.
    - A settled question still pays some empirical science packs: more on a product whose trial costs more ore, up to a limit, and more on products of the process each map makes richest in science. The Coverage tab shows it per product, once a question there is settled. On new and existing maps.
    - Research data is renamed Empirical science pack, with a new icon: a mint flask.
    - New Notebook tab: one page of notes, shared by the force and kept with the map.
    - The station's circuit signals are explained in a new tip, Station signals, instead of a table in the station window.
    - The Findings tab shows when each finding was completed.
    - A higher quality analysis station analyses faster, as a lab researches faster.
    - Twelve new achievements.
    - New Progress tab: samples analysed by science pack, empirical science packs made, packs per sample, questions settled, findings, products made with a dose and extra products from doses, over game time, with each finding marked.
    - New tips: Sample kinds, Empirical science packs, Fluid hoppers, Temperature, Findings, Ripeness, Scrap samples and Quality additives.
    - Many descriptions and tooltips are shorter and plainer.
    - Tips and tricks entries have an icon in front of their title.
    - First experiment, Samples, Dosing production, Residues and Fluid hoppers in Tips and tricks show a working scene.
    - New startup setting, Empirical science packs in endless technologies, on by default. Off, no endless technology costs them.
    - Trial recipes carry the test bench in their icon's corner, so a trial's signal no longer looks like its product's. The Experiments tab has the test bench as its icon.
    - Mixtures tab: a fluid or biological member of the pair has a temperature or ripeness selector. The surface shows the results at the chosen bands, and each tested mixture names its bands.
    - The fluid chiller is a cooling tower: Space Age's heating tower in blue, 3x3, with a cold glow while it burns a charge. Fluid passes through its middle from west to east and leaves north.
    - A test bench takes a fluid ingredient in the middle of its top edge, as assembling machine 2 does. Hoppers dose from its other edge tiles.
    - A discovered activation or premix is announced as "Recipe unlocked".
    - On the Mixtures tab, hovering a Synergy shows its premix, or how many samples each of the four groups has against the number it needs.
    - The pressure heater is painted crimson, and its icon carries tungsten carbide: it looked exactly like the fluid heater.
    - Hoppers show a dose being handed out: an item hopper's hatch closes and opens, a fluid hopper's pump turns.
    - Theory tab: predictions too uncertain to rank anything are left out of Where to look next, and families with no results on a process show a dash.
  Bugfixes:
    - Without the Quality mod, analysis stations never took a science pack, so no sample was ever analysed.
    - With a lot of evidence, opening the Empirical Science screen or changing its sort froze the game for up to a minute, and judging findings caused a pause every ten seconds while doses were made. Each product's results are now indexed once and reused until new evidence arrives.
    - Empirical science packs, samples and scrap samples weighed a tenth of a science pack: 10,000 fitted in a rocket. They now fit 1,000, as science packs do.
    - Modules and beacons had no effect on test benches. Speed modules now speed up trials.
    - On the Screening grid, rows for alternative recipes were one cell short, which shifted their values one column left.
    - A premix could stay locked after its synergy was proven, until one more sample of the mixture itself was analysed. Samples of each additive alone and of the control now unlock it too.
    - The Best composition tip's scene did not build: its example blueprint held a station layout that only fitted with Space Age.
    - Premixes and activated residues with results were missing from the Empirical Science screen until their recipe was unlocked.
    - The Mixtures tab plotted a heated or chilled mixture with the same doses at other temperatures, as if repeated, and compared it with its members at their usual temperature.
    - A mixture with a heated, chilled or ripened member could never unlock its premix. It now counts against its members alone at the same band.
    - Extra products of a dosed machine that makes something perishable could come out fresh when the machine had no ingredients left waiting. They now come out as fresh as the products they join.
    - The Station port tip showed an error instead of its scene.
    - The Empirical Science page of Tips and tricks showed an unknown key instead of its text.
